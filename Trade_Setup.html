Your Decision Flow (Swing Strategy + Professional Checklist)

Pre-Trade Checklist

  • Global Sentiment
  • HTF Trend
  • Key Levels Marked
  • Opening Analysis
  • Market Structure
  • Volume Confirmation
  • Option Chain
  • Sector Strength
  • Risk:Reward ≥ 1:2
  • Psychology Ready

Time Frame Guide

  • 9:15 - 9:30 Opening & Market Direction
  • 9:30 - 10:30 Look for First High Probability Setup
  • 10:30 - 1:00 Trade with Trend, Avoid Overtrading
  • 1:00 - 2:00 Lower Volatility (Be Selective)
  • 2:00 - 3:15 Trend Continuation or Reversal

Market Phase

  • BULLISH: Use CE Setup
  • BEARISH: Use PE Setup
  • RANGE: Avoid / Wait for Breakout

1. Global Market Sentiment

Gift Nifty, US Market, Asia Market, DXY, Crude, Bond Yield

→ Set Market Bias (Bullish / Bearish / Neutral)

2. Higher Time Frame Trend

Daily → 1H → 15m Trend

→ Only trade in the direction of trend

3. Mark Key Levels

PDH, PDL, Weekly High/Low, VWAP, Swing Levels

→ Identify Important Support & Resistance

4. Opening Analysis

Gap Up/Down, Opening Range, First 15 min High/Low, ORB

→ Understand Early Market Direction & Control

5. Market Structure

HH-HL or LH-LL, Break of Structure (BOS)

→ Confirm Buyers or Sellers in Control

6. Volume Confirmation

Breakout Volume, Pullback Volume, Volume Spike/Dry-up

→ Confirm Participation & Strength

7. Option Chain Confirmation

OI Change, PCR, Max Pain, Call/Put Writer Positioning

→ Confirm Market Positioning & Sentiment

8. Sector Strength

Banking, Financials, IT (Especially for NIFTY)

→ Strong Sectors Lead the Index
⌛ WAIT FOR SETUP: Do not force a trade. Wait for high probability setup.

PE Premium Expanding

15. Risk Management

SL Below Swing (CE) / Above Swing (PE) • Risk:Reward ≥ 1:2 • Proper Position Sizing

16. Trade Management

Trail SL, Partial Booking, Let Winners Run

17. Review & Journal

Record Trade, Mistakes, Learning, Improve Consistency

Why This Flow Works

  • Trend Direction Confirms Bias
  • Levels Give High Probability
  • Volume Confirms Participation
  • Option Chain Shows Smart Money
  • Swing Setup Gives Perfect Entry
  • Risk Management Protects Capital

Important Rules

  • Only trade in the direction of Higher Time Frame Trend.
  • Always wait for setup at key levels.
  • No setup = No trade. Patience is an edge.
  • Never risk more than 1-2% of capital in a single trade.
  • Cut losses short, let profits run.
  • Follow process, not emotions.
